About the road data

Solid brown roads use Riverside County’s maintained dirt/gravel inventory. County maintenance does not establish bike or foot permission.

Dashed brown roads have an explicit unpaved tag in OpenStreetMap and align with Caltrans road geometry. Alignment is evidence, not a public-access guarantee.

Forest road colors describe surfaces. Motor-vehicle designations do not establish bike or foot permission. Check local signs and current closures.

Coverage is incomplete. An empty area does not mean there are no public roads.

Angeles, Modoc, and Six Rivers have limited road data here; use their official maps.
